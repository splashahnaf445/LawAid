CLIENT & AUTHORIZED PERSONMetro Housing Ltd.(MD. Imtiaz - Managing Director)
Case Details & Narrative

Land dispute case involving Metro Housing Ltd. and the Land Ministry. The hearing is scheduled for the next date as mentioned above. The petitioner asserts lawful ownership and freehold title over 4.8 acres of urban commercial land under Registry Deed #7821/18, wrongfully demarcated as government gazette acquisition without statutory notice.

The primary prayers before the Hon'ble High Court include an injunction against arbitrary boundary demarcations, an expedited reassessment of the jurisdictional Land Acquisition survey map, and revocation of the summary eviction directive served on 4th January 2024.
