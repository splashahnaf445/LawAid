# Feasibility Analysis & SWOT

## Feasibility

| Dimension | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Technical** | The stack (React/Next.js, Flutter, Node.js/Express, FastAPI, PostgreSQL) is mature. AI, payments, maps and storage use third-party APIs, so risk is low. The real challenge is securely integrating many services. |
| **Economic** | Third-party APIs keep upfront cost low. 78% of respondents prefer sessions under 1,000 BDT, which supports a consultation-fee model. Main risks: lawyer acquisition, marketing to build trust from zero, and variable AI API cost. |
| **Operational** | Strong demand: 86.4% found AI matching useful and 89.8% wanted the SOS feature. A 64.4% client / 35.6% lawyer split supports the dual-portal design. Trust risk: 49.2% cited privacy and 42.4% payment concerns, addressed by verification and escrow. |
| **Legal** | Verification aligns with Bar Council of Bangladesh standards; the chatbot is scoped to general information with a disclaimer. As a payment intermediary it must follow Bangladesh financial and data-protection rules. Review with legal counsel before commercial launch. |
| **Schedule** | Proposal, benchmark, survey and SWOT were delivered as sequential Agile phases. A modular system allows an MVP first (AI chatbot, verified search, video consultation). |

## SWOT

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | All-in-one legal platform · verified lawyer profiles · AI-powered matching · bilingual AI assistant · multiple consultation options · strong case management · secure, transparent payments |
| **Weaknesses** | Cannot fully guarantee lawyer service quality · AI answers require careful control |
| **Opportunities** | Large untapped digital legal market · high dissatisfaction with existing methods · digitization of traditional referrals · growing demand for online services |
| **Threats** | Traditional referral habits · competition from existing platforms |

**Strategy:** use AI features, verified profiles, online consultation and secure payments to modernize traditional legal services, focusing on trust, simplicity and accurate AI assistance to reduce the main weaknesses and threats.
