# Benchmark & Gap Analysis

Benchmarked **12 features** against **5 platforms** (UkilBD, FindMyAdvocate, PathLegal, LawRato, DoNotPay), exceeding the minimum of 2n = 10 features.

![Benchmark table](images/benchmark-table.png)

**Result:** no competitor combines verified consultation, case management and public legal literacy in one system.

## Gap analysis

| Feature area | Current state (competitors) | Gap | LawAid's response |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI lawyer matching | Only 1 of 5 (FindMyAdvocate) | Most rely on manual search | AI matching by specialization, location, rating and fee |
| In-app video/audio calling | Partial only (LawRato) | Users leave the platform to consult remotely | Native in-app video, audio and real-time chat |
| Case management dashboard | Only 1 of 5 (PathLegal) | No integrated way to track cases/hearings | Full dashboard for lawyers and clients |
| Payment + QR verification | None full; partial (LawRato) | No fraud-resistant verifiable payment | Escrow payments with QR-verified receipts |
| Public AI legal chatbot | Only 1 of 5 (DoNotPay), no marketplace | No platform pairs literacy with lawyer access | Bilingual AI assistant inside the same platform |
| Bangla + English support | Partial at best | No platform fully serves bilingual users | Full bilingual support |
| Confidential misconduct reporting | None | No accountability mechanism | Confidential reporting alongside public ratings |
